Project Overview

Bridged real-world evidence (dashcam footage + FARO Focus Core 3D laser scans) with driving simulators for forensic VR accident reconstruction. Imported high-fidelity point cloud environments into IPG CarMaker, integrated a Skoda Octavia vehicle model, and aligned virtual scenarios with real-world coordinates via OpenStreetMap data. Also explored CARLA as a parallel digital twin platform.

Key Contributions

  • 3D Scene Capture: Acquired point cloud data with FARO Focus Core laser scanner (±2 mm accuracy)
  • Simulation Pipeline: Imported scanned 3D environments into IPG CarMaker for driving simulation
  • Vehicle Integration: Added Skoda Octavia model with dashcam perspective for scenario replay
  • Geo-Alignment: Aligned virtual scenario with real-world coordinates via OpenStreetMap
  • CARLA Digital Twin: Explored open-source CARLA simulator as an alternative reconstruction platform

Built an end-to-end data engineering pipeline handling 32 petabytes of raw vehicle bus data and camera outputs. The pipeline extracts relevant sensor signals, applies computer vision algorithms to detect and temporally track dynamic objects (vehicles, pedestrians, cyclists), and reconstructs the full driving scenarios inside a simulation environment — enabling automated validation of ADAS functions at massive scale.

VENTURE CONCEPT · WORKING PROTOTYPE · EXIST PREPARATION

Scope / Renewal Studio

Connecting insurance renewal answers to the evidence behind them.

The problem

Commercial insurance renewals involve documents, company figures, and multiple questionnaires. When one figure changes, a broker has to understand which answers need another review.

The approach

Scope connects source passages, contextual facts, questionnaire answers, and human approvals. A change to relevant evidence reopens the affected answers while retaining their review history.

  • Evidence-linked facts with company, reporting period, and actual-versus-forecast context.
  • Proposed document extraction and questionnaire mapping with explicit review.
  • A dependency graph, selective approval invalidation, and a traceable activity history.
  • Prepared follow-up drafts and review-gated export packs.

Current stage: a working prototype using synthetic demonstration data, with venture research and EXIST application preparation underway.
