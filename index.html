AKKODIS / THESIS GRADE 1.0 / 2026

UAV landing research

A reproducible simulation benchmark for landing on a moving platform.

Implementation

Built the landing task in Gazebo with PX4 SITL and ROS 2 integration. Trained and compared PPO, SAC and TD3 with PyTorch, Stable-Baselines3 and Gymnasium. The evaluation included reward design, reactive controller comparisons, CBF experiments, TensorBoard monitoring and standardized trajectory and metrics logs.

Simulation result

PPO achieved 5.5 cm mean final horizontal error across 100 evaluation episodes. This was a controlled Gazebo/PX4 SITL benchmark with one training seed.

Data and evaluation

Recorded position, target state, control behaviour and episode outcomes for repeatable comparisons.

Hardware platform

Separate Pixhawk assembly and integration work provided a platform for future physical policy testing.

VENTURE CONCEPT · WORKING PROTOTYPE · EXIST PREPARATION

Scope / Renewal Studio

Connecting insurance renewal answers to the evidence behind them.

Work
Workflow design & backend
Domain
Commercial insurance renewal
Stage
Prototype / EXIST preparation

A change should have a traceable consequence

A commercial insurance renewal brings together source documents, company figures and several questionnaires. When a figure changes, the reviewer needs to see which answers depend on it and which approvals need another look.

  1. SourceA document passage and its origin
  2. ContextCompany, period and fact type
  3. ReviewAn answer linked to its evidence
  4. ApproveA reviewed, traceable export

Backend integrity

The TypeScript API and D1-backed state model connect scoped facts, source passages, revision checks and answer approvals. A relevant change reopens the affected answers while preserving their activity history. Account-scoped persistence and transactional writes keep updates consistent.

Human review by design

Extraction and questionnaire mapping produce proposals. Reviewers inspect the evidence, correct the context and approve the result. Selective invalidation helps them focus on the answers affected by a change.

A working prototype using synthetic demonstration data. Customer validation and venture research are ongoing, including EXIST application preparation.
